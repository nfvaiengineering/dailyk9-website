Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026

Daily K9 Academy is a service of Daily K9 Academy LLC, an Arizona limited liability company operating in Maricopa County, Arizona (“Daily K9,” “we,” “us,” “our”). This policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, who else can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

We train service dog teams. That means some of what you tell us touches on your health and disability. We treat that information as the sensitive material it is, and this policy spells out exactly how.

What we collect

Information you give us directly. When you book an intake call, complete a client intake form, upload vaccination records, message us through the chat window on this site, or email or call us, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, and — if you give it — your home or training address
  • Information about your dog: name, breed, age, sex, spay/neuter status, origin, behavior history, and current training level
  • Your training goals, preferred locations, and scheduling availability
  • Veterinary and vaccination records you choose to upload
  • Photos and video, where you have given consent

Disability-related information. For service dog work specifically, you may choose to tell us about a disability so that we can help you identify and train tasks that mitigate it. You are never required to disclose a diagnosis to work with us. We need to understand what the dog must do, not what your medical file says. Anything you do share is used only to plan and deliver training.

A note on the chat window. The chat window on this site is a convenience for quick questions. Please do not type medical details or diagnoses into it — tell us what you are looking for and we will take the rest to a private intake call.

Information collected automatically. Our website records standard technical information — IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring site — through our hosting provider and our analytics tool. This is aggregate traffic information; we do not use it to build a profile of you.

How we use your information

  • To assess whether your dog is a suitable prospect and to tell you honestly if it is not
  • To plan, schedule and deliver training sessions
  • To track your team’s progress toward public-access and task standards
  • To confirm vaccination status before in-person work, for the safety of every dog and handler we work with
  • To invoice you and administer payment plans
  • To contact you about your training, and — only if you have opted in — about news or offerings
  • To publish a testimonial, photo or video, only where you have given specific written consent, and only in the form you agreed to

Who else can see your information

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else’s advertising. We use a small number of service providers who process information on our behalf:

  • Netlify — website hosting and form handling. Processes IP addresses and server request logs, and receives and stores the intake forms and vaccination records you submit through this site.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — site analytics. Cookieless and aggregated; does not track individuals across sites.
  • Square — payment processing. Processes name, email, billing details and card data. Card numbers are handled by Square and are not stored by Daily K9 Academy.
  • HandlerSystems, LLC (handlersystems.com) — live chat. Processes whatever a visitor types into the chat, which may include health or disability information. HandlerSystems, LLC is a separate Arizona limited liability company under common ownership with Daily K9 Academy LLC.

Beyond those providers, we disclose information only where we are legally required to, or where disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent harm to a person or an animal.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information
  • We do not share your disability information with anyone outside Daily K9 without your explicit permission
  • We do not require medical documentation to begin training
  • We do not issue, sell or endorse service dog “registrations,” “certifications of registry,” or ID cards from online registries. No such registry is recognized under the Americans with Disabilities Act or Arizona law
  • We do not post photos, video or testimonials featuring you or your dog without your specific consent

How long we keep your information

We keep active client records for the duration of your program and for seven years afterward, which covers the useful working life of a service dog team and any follow-up support you may need. Vaccination records are kept for the duration of training plus one year. Intake enquiries that do not become client relationships are deleted within twenty-four months. You can ask us to delete your information sooner — see below.

Service dog work and sensitive information

Arizona law and the Americans with Disabilities Act both limit what anyone may ask a handler about their service animal. We hold ourselves to the spirit of that: we ask what a trainer needs in order to train, and nothing more.

If you have shared disability information with us and later want it removed from your file, tell us and we will remove it while keeping the training records we need to continue working with your dog.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information, subject to any records we must keep by law
  • Withdraw consent for photos, video or testimonials — we will remove the material from anything we control
  • Stop sending you marketing email

Email nv@dailyk9.com and we will respond within thirty days. There is no charge for this.

Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under thirteen. Where a minor participates in training as part of a household, we collect only what the parent or guardian provides. If you believe we hold information about a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reputable providers, keep access limited to those who need it, and transmit information over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise — but if a breach ever affects your personal information, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and add a “last updated” date. Material changes affecting how we handle disability information or veterinary records will be emailed to active clients directly.
