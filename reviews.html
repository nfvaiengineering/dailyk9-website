We got homework :-) and it became very clear how important those sessions were. Throughout, Nicole adapted the plan and techniques to get us to our goal on our wedding day. We had so much fun! We're both so proud of what this team accomplished — our girls learned to hold a bouquet in their mouth, carry it to a basket and drop the flowers, then go to a specific place to sit.
